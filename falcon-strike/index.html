Bank to turn — coordinated turns come from roll, not rudder. Watch the G-meter: above 6 G your vision fades. Keep airspeed above 150 kts or you will sink. Hold a bandit in the reticle until LOCK, then fire: IR (T) locks fast but dies at range, radar (M) locks out to 3 km. Red blips on radar are bandits. Kill them before they find you.
